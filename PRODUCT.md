# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TLLCG is for two local players sharing one screen and reading the same table from opposite sides of the match. Players need fast confirmation of turn, phase, resources, hero health, board slots, card states, targeting, and recent effects without one side receiving better information than the other.

## Product Purpose

The game should make the full data-driven card system playable and trustworthy: players can choose factions, start a match, play units, artifacts, and spells, resolve effects, advance turns, and clearly understand why the board changed.

## Brand Personality

Tactical, legible, sharp. The interface should feel like a competitive tabletop card surface with enough atmosphere to feel like a game, but never at the expense of rules clarity.

## Anti-references

Avoid collectible-card-game spectacle that hides state, giant decorative panels that compress the actual board, single-player-biased HUDs, unreadable dark-on-dark controls, tiny hero health, and any generated card or hero artwork. No overlapping UI, no hidden required controls, no one-sided information hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. The table is the product: board slots, hands, heroes, resources, and targeting must stay visually dominant.
2. Equal players, equal clarity: both sides get equivalent hero/resource/status treatment.
3. Every state change should leave evidence through animation, log text, or visible counters.
4. Polish should reinforce rules, not decorate over them.
5. Debug tools may exist, but they should stay visually secondary to the match.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast where practical in Godot UI, stable large touch/click targets, readable labels at 1080p, color plus text/icon status cues, and reduced-motion-friendly feedback that does not block play.

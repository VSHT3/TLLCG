## Ability
Counter 0.  
At the start of your turn, roll `dice: d20|render`. 
- If you roll 20, heal 2 and boost 2 to your hero. 
	- Else, the number corresponds to card slots on your side of the board. 
		- If a unit is present on the slot, heal it by 1. 
			- If Sir Umm is on the slot, also increase it’s counter by 1. 
		- Else, you may pay 3 to apply poison to a non-hero unit.

At the end of your turn, deal counter damage to a non-hero unit.
